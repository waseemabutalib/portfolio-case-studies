# DHC Infrastructure and Client Delivery

## Overview

At the University of Houston Digital Humanities Core, I support web infrastructure, digital publishing platforms, workshops, documentation, 3D scanning workflows, and client-facing technical delivery for research and student projects.

This work sits between implementation, project coordination, platform support, stakeholder communication, training, and practical technical problem-solving.

## Context

The Digital Humanities Core supports faculty, students, and research projects that need digital infrastructure, web publishing tools, workshops, and technical support. Many projects involve translating research goals into practical platform setup, access, documentation, training, and delivery workflows.

## What I Did

- Supported 4+ active departmental website projects, including research sites and professional faculty sites.
- Coordinated access, permissions, resources, meetings, and communication between faculty stakeholders, designers, programmers, students, and project teams.
- Facilitated project teams ranging from 1:1 technical support to 4-8 person working groups, with larger cross-team meetings involving 20+ participants.
- Conducted recurring OCR support sessions twice weekly over several months, helping participants convert scanned documents, PDFs, and image-based text into searchable and editable data.
- Supported workshops, classroom sessions, and hybrid events across ArcGIS, ClioVis, OCR, podcast production, AI tools, 3D scanning, web publishing, UX/UI, and cloud/web platforms.
- Evaluated an AI digital humanities platform, documented strengths, limitations, and teaching use cases, and presented findings to teaching and learning stakeholders.
- Created documentation for podcast workflows, hybrid presentation setup, AI platform evaluation, after-action reports, summer coverage planning, project coordination, and onboarding/support processes.
- Prepared 3D scanning workshop materials by scanning 6 physical figures into digital 3D assets and supporting Blender-based review and presentation workflows.

## Project Flow

1. Understand the stakeholder, project type, and expected deliverable.
2. Identify the platform, toolset, access, or workflow needed.
3. Set up or coordinate access, permissions, meetings, and resources.
4. Support designers, programmers, students, or faculty through meetings and technical guidance.
5. Document workflows where repeat use or handoff is likely.
6. Support delivery through training, troubleshooting, workshop facilitation, or project handoff.
